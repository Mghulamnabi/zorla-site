Terms & Conditions

These terms explain how Zorla works and what you can expect from us, from our service partners, and what we ask from you. By creating an account or booking a service on Zorla, you agree to these terms.

1. What Zorla is

Zorla is a home-services platform operated by Hunain Tech Solution, Lahore, Pakistan. Zorla connects customers with independent, identity-verified service partners (installers and technicians) for installation, repair and maintenance work at homes and offices across Pakistan.

2. Fixed prices, shown before you book

Every service on Zorla shows its fixed price before you book. The price you see at the time of booking is the price you pay for the work listed in that service, including the items listed under "Includes". If a job needs extra work or materials that are not included, the partner must explain and agree the extra cost with you before starting that extra work.

3. Payment

Payment is made in cash on completion: you check the finished work and then pay the partner the amount shown on your booking and receipt. Please do not pay in advance for a standard booking.

4. The 10% platform share

Zorla keeps a platform share of 10% out of the service price. This share comes out of the booked price — it is never added on top for the customer. The split is printed openly on every receipt: on a Rs 3,000 job, Rs 2,700 goes to the partner and Rs 300 is the platform share.

5. The 7-day service guarantee

Workmanship on every completed job is covered for 7 days from the day the job is completed. If the same fault comes back within those 7 days, tell us through the complaint option in the Zorla app, or call the Zorla helpline 0321-1680194 — and the partner will re-visit and put the work right without a labour charge. The guarantee covers the work that was done; it does not cover new faults, damage from misuse, or parts and materials that you bought yourself.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

You may cancel or reschedule a booking free of charge at any time before the partner leaves for your address. If you must cancel later, please inform us as early as you can. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may limit future bookings.

7. What we ask of partners

Partners on Zorla are independent professionals whose identity we verify by CNIC. A partner must arrive on time, bring his own tools, use his own materials unless the booked service lists materials as included, behave respectfully in your home, and charge only the booked price (plus any extra you agreed in advance under section 2). Partners remit the 10% platform share to Zorla from the amounts they collect.

8. What we ask of customers

Please give a correct address and a phone number where you can be reached, be present at the booked time (or arrange for someone to be there), and pay the agreed amount when the job is complete.

9. Our responsibility

Zorla works to match you with a verified partner and stands behind the 7-day guarantee above. If something goes wrong with a booking, our responsibility is limited to the amount paid for that booking. Nothing in these terms takes away any right you have under the laws of Pakistan.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms from time to time; the current version is always the one shown here and in the Zorla apps. Questions about these terms: Hunain Tech Solution, Lahore — please use the Help / support option inside the Zorla app, or call the Zorla helpline 0321-1680194.
