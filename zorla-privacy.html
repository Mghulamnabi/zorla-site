Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in plain words, what information Zorla collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Zorla is operated by Hunain Tech Solution, Lahore, Pakistan.

1. What we collect

From customers: your name, phone number, the address where the service is to be done, and your booking details (service, time, price and job history).

From partners: your name, phone number, CNIC number, your trade, your working area and your years of experience.

2. Why we collect it

  • To take your booking and send the right partner to the right address.
  • To verify a partner's identity by CNIC, so customers can trust who is coming to their home.
  • To show your receipts, job history and guarantee status.
  • To contact you about your booking — for example if a partner is running late.
  • To improve our services and keep the platform safe and fair.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. A partner sees only the details needed for your booking (such as your address and phone number for that job). A customer sees a partner's name, trade, rating and work profile — never the partner's CNIC.

4. Who holds your information

Your information stays with Hunain Tech Solution, the operator of Zorla. It is not passed to any other company except where the law requires it.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep booking records while your account is active, so your receipts and guarantee claims remain available. You may ask us to delete your personal information at any time — use the Help / support option inside the Zorla app, or call the Zorla helpline 0321-1680194, and we will delete it, except for any record we are required to keep for accounts or legal reasons.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your information: Hunain Tech Solution, Lahore — please use the Help / support option inside the Zorla app, or call the Zorla helpline 0321-1680194.
